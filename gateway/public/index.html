Usage Guidelines, Rules & Regulations

  • Permitted Use: Educational evaluation, internship review, API/security testing, and architectural demonstration.
  • Fair Rate Limits: Endpoints enforce sliding-window rate limiting (HTTP 429) to prevent denial-of-service abuse.
  • Privacy & DPDP/GDPR: Do not enter real sensitive credentials or personal financial data; emails and 12-digit IDs are masked automatically before logging.
  • Responsible Disclosure: Report security bugs privately per SECURITY.md in the repository.

Known System Limitations & Disclaimers

  • Free-Tier Cloud Hosting: Render free instances spin down after 15 minutes of inactivity; the first request may experience a 20–50 second cold start.
  • Demo Storage Persistence: The public gateway uses in-memory stores by default so data resets on restart unless live PostgreSQL (DATABASE_URL) and Redis (REDIS_URL) are attached.
  • AI Decision Advisory: The Cognitive Brain enforces deterministic OPA/guardrail rules and flags high-risk actions for Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) review; it is not legal or financial advice.
